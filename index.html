WHO WE ARE
The Intergalactic Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (IGACDL) is an esteemed coalition of defense advocates whose reach spans across star systems, planetary colonies, orbital habitats, and warp-capable civilizations.

We work to ensure that constitutional, interstellar, and sentient-rights protections extend to all beings — human, alien, synthetic, temporal, or otherwise — wherever justice is threatened.

Rooted in the centuries-old traditions of criminal defense and strengthened by emerging interplanetary legal doctrines, IGACDL stands as the premier legal organization representing defenders throughout the Milky Way and beyond.

Mission Statement

The mission of the Intergalactic Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (IGACDL) is to serve as a leader in identifying and reforming flaws and inequities in criminal prosecutions by planetary governments, federated systems, or extraterrestrial tribunals; promote principles of interstellar due process and multi-species legal respect; share specialized knowledge on cross-planetary criminal procedure; train defenders to handle cases involving dimensional anomalies, warp-travel jurisdictional issues, and non-human legal systems; and maintain justice as a constant, even as civilizations expand across the cosmos.

Through unwavering dedication, IGACDL ensures justice reaches the farthest corners of space.
